Overview

This is my submission for a video contest. The rules were that any given submission must be under 1 minute and 30 seconds, have only inanimate objects as characters, and have no audio beyond a single song for a soundtrack, or SFX as needed.

Project Details


Type: Short Film

Tools: Adobe Premiere Pro

Year: 2023

Concept

I had been experimenting with 3D printing before this contest, and felt confident in my ability to print out something humanoid. I had found out about the "rocktopus" culture in the 3D printing world around the same time. It is the idea of using a model of Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson's head in strange applications and models. Giving Dwayne's head an octopus form was the first of such applications which coined the term.

I thought it would be a cool idea to take a Toy Story like approach. And I also wanted to highlight the 3D printed nature of the rocktopus, so I anthropomorphised my rocktopus to be lonely, and to dedicate his time to 3D printing a partner for himself while I'm gone from the house.
