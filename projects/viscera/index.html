Overview

Visceras is a video component for an ongoing prop-creation project. It introduces the world that this prop would live in. A world where mushroom spores have adapted to grow in copper wires and infect the power grid until it compounds into an apocalyptic level catastrophe, as power stations around the world explode into mushgroom clouds. Without technology to aid mankind, how will we survive?

Project Details


Type: Short Film

Tools: Adobe After Effects

Year: 2025

Concept

The video component of this project makes use of many themes. Each act is shown not through individuals in this world, but relevant radio communications. Due to amateaur radio's independence from the wider grid, some remain safe - for now.

On a high level, the concept of this project is to highlight how disconnected we've become from our sense of touch. Human touch, to be exact. Because all of our HIDs (Human input devices) involve touching glass, plastic, rubber or metal, society at large has had a strange alienation from itself, and my theory is that part of our inability to connect like we used to is partially due to our main means of physical interactions being filtered through cold, unfeeling, industrial materials. This project is aimed at creating a piece of technology who's sole means of interaction is to make physical contact with a living being. This is achieved through mushrooms, who are otherwise hardy, stable, and easy to read electrical changes from.

Leveraging these advantages to have this living being "respond" to our inputs in an organic way is something that breaks some of these barriers down. The "mushroom god", as I refer to it, is positioned as a being with higher power, a deity that is able to enact it's sentience via the technology we have access to to day. It is contained to a Bentley Portable Television, and just enough control over this self-contained device to interact with us too.
