Kodak

Ad Campaign

Overview

This project is my attempt at an ad campaign for a company seeking to reclaim it's former cultural and market dominance - a new angle on the familiar.

Project Details


Client: Kodak (Academic Project)

Medium: Digital Billboard Design

Tools: Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop

Year: 2024

Concept

With Gen Z now more interested in nostalgia than ever, and their preference for experiences, Kodak, now more than ever, has an opportunity to recapture the hearts of the youth with the magic of film photography, and physical media. By contrasting the romantisism of instant cameras with the absurdity of what most of our camera roll looks like today, I can highlight the art of photography against the unending, impersonal wave of accidental screenshots, QR codes and grocery lists we have learned to live with in the digital age.

A theme I will also be leaning into heavily for this is simplicity, and physical touch. By extension, I will also highlight the limitations of digital photos to exaggerate them. Having a real, physical, photo as an object is something we relate to and interact with much differently than we do our phone screens. We put physical images in the real world with us; in our wallets, on our walls, in our scrapbooks, on the fridge, and so on.
