Overview

This is an experimental title sequence inspired by Saul Bass' approach tomovement and motion graphics for a fictional show.

Project Details


Type: Title Sequence

Medium: Film/Video

Tools: Adobe After Effects

Year: 2025

Concept

The fictional show or movie this would represent is a soviet-era spy movie. With Saul Bass, Constructivism and the technology of the soviet era as influences, I created this project with the express intent of trying to only use typography as the basis for the motion design. I chose the prevaling theme to be infrared, as it is both reflective of the technology of the time, and inherets a lot of the soviet and constructivist design langauges, as well as the physical properties of infrared light - invisible to the naked eye.
